## Interview 1: AI Deployment Manager (30 min) — your future ADM partner

They're evaluating: *would this SA make my accounts easier or harder?*

- Lead with the partnership operating model: you run point, they get evidence and
  precise, cheap asks; weekly async; you never surprise them in front of a customer.
- Know the ADM's world: quota on renewals/expansion across many accounts, needs
  champions and proof points, allergic to SAs who create meetings.
- Have 2–3 stories ready: a technical engagement turned into commercial evidence; a
  skeptical exec managed; ownership handed to a customer team and walked away from.
- Good questions to ask them: "What does your best SA partnership look like
  day-to-day?" "Where do engagements on your accounts usually stall — technical,
  political, or procurement?" "How do you capture value evidence today, and what's
  missing?"

## Interview 2: Senior leader, Solutions Architect team (30 min)

They're evaluating: judgment, altitude, and whether you scale beyond one account.

- Be ready for: "Walk me through the Acme plan in 3 minutes" (the one-sentence answer
  from `README.md` + the three decisions + metrics + champion ladder), then drilling
  on *why* — why that wedge, why those metrics, what you'd cut with 30 days instead
  of 90.
- Software-factory vision at the org level: how one team's paved road becomes 18
  teams' operating model, and what the SA org should productize from it (playbooks,
  template repos, the reusable pilot kit from the leadership asks).
- Market honesty: know the Codex/Claude Code comparison cold and answer without trash
  talk — senior leaders test whether you're credible in front of a CTO.
- Good questions: "What separates your top-quartile SAs from the middle?" "How much of
  the role is greenfield adoption vs renewal defense today?" "What's the SA org's
  biggest product gap for enterprise evidence/ROI storytelling?"
